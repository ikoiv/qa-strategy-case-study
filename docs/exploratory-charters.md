# Exploratory charters

| Charter | Timebox | Explore | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Status honesty | 45 minutes | Delay, reject, redeliver, and reorder integration responses; refresh/open two tabs | Request IDs, timelines, UI/core state comparison |
| Data boundaries | 45 minutes | Unicode, whitespace, long addresses, empty optional fields, duplicate member names | Exact input/stored values and unexpected transformations |
| Staff recovery | 30 minutes | Lose network after submit, retry, return later, follow an exception | Whether staff can tell what happened and what to do next |
| Keyboard and errors | 30 minutes | Navigate the complete workflow without a mouse; inspect focus and error association with a screen reader | Session notes, observed barriers, reproducible steps |

These are investigation goals, not pass/fail scripts. Record discoveries, unanswered questions, and new cases. A completed charter is evidence of a session, not a full accessibility or resilience certification.
